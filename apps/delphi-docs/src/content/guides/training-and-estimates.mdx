Every model is fit by the same trainer, so the settings below apply to all of
them, from the [Quickstart](/quickstart)'s `Median` to every truncated model.

## Hyperparameters

**Hyperparameters are a keyword argument.** Every model runs on its config's
defaults; override any of them with `hyperparameters={"iterations": 5000}`.
Each class's page lists its config's keys and defaults under
*Hyperparameters*, its own first and the shared ones folded below.

**Hyperparameters are a plain dict**, validated against a pydantic config.
Keys the config does not declare are ignored with a warning, so a misspelled
key is audible rather than silent.

**The Fisher schedule is the default** for the truncated models. It sets the
step size from the Hessian of the loss, starting at $1 / L$ and decaying as
$1 / (L + \mu t)$, and re-measures both as the fit moves, so neither needs
tuning. Pass `hyperparameters={"scheduler": None}` for a constant rate.

**Some families precondition.** Families whose sufficient statistics are
strongly correlated on the truncation set, such as the inverse Gaussian's $x$
and $1/x$ or the two-parameter Gamma's $\log x$ and $x$, set `precondition`:
each step multiplies the gradient by the inverse covariance of the sufficient
statistics over the observed sample, the truncated NLL's Hessian at the truth.

## Estimates

**Use the `avg_` estimates.** Every model exposes four: `avg_` (the mean of
the iterates after a burn-in, set by `avg_burn_in`), `ema_` (a bias-corrected
moving average), `final_` (the last iterate) and `best_` (the iterate with the
lowest validation loss). The averaged iterate is the one the convergence
guarantees are about; `best_` is the least reliable of the four in our tests.
A model built directly on the trainer reads the same four from its run, as
`avg_params`, `ema_params`, `final_params` and `best_params`.

**`model.summary()` reports how a fit went**: the data, the optimizer,
scheduler, steps, stop reason and final losses, all four estimates and the
curvature at the estimate. Truncated models add the survival mass, how often
the projection fired, and the fitted model's own mass on the truncation set (a
mismatch with `alpha` means the fit has gone wrong). It has no standard errors
or p-values. Estimates are reported in the coordinates the model was fit in.

## Logging

**Logging is off until you turn it on.** delphi.ai logs through the standard
library under the `delphi` logger and installs no handler:

```python
import logging

logging.basicConfig(level=logging.INFO)
logging.getLogger("delphi.trainer").setLevel(logging.WARNING)  # per module
```
